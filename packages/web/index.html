Whisk

Whisk is an email client for your Gmail with your calendar built in. Read, search, write, and organize your mail: archive, label, star, snooze, and send. Whisk runs as a Mac app, in any browser, and on iPhone, and keeps them all in step.

Download Whisk for Mac

Whisk syncs with Gmail on your behalf and keeps your mailbox on your device, so the app renders instantly and works offline. Your mail is used only to provide the client to you — never for advertising, never sold, never used to train AI models.
